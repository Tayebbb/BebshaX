# BebshaX — Datasets Documentation

> Single source of truth: `scripts/dataset_manifest.py`. Generated by `scripts/setup_datasets.py`.
> Legacy datasets serve grounding, diversity, behavioral examples, and evaluation only. The owner-approved R9 exception (2026-09-08) permits isolated non-LLM training on explicitly reviewed public synthetic data in `ml_persona`. **No LLM fine-tuning.** No real PII, private studies, uploads or conversations in training.

## Profile Hierarchy

Profiles follow a strict subset relationship: `minimal` ⊂ `development` ⊂ `evaluation` ⊂ `full`.

- **`minimal` (< 1 GB):** Persona diversity seeds, dialogue examples, and health probes (`personahub_sample`, `synthetic_persona_chat`, `mmlu_micro`, `gsm8k_micro`). **No grounding corpora** — with only this profile the evidence store is empty and every persona claim is honestly INFERRED/SYNTHETIC (never fabricated OBSERVED).
- **`development` (< 5 GB):** Minimal + the citable grounding corpora — real business review evidence and empathetic dialogues (`amazon_reviews_office_products`, `empathetic_dialogues_slice`).
- **`evaluation` (< 5 GB):** Development + router benchmark evaluation suites (`router_arena`, `xroute_bench`).
- **`full` (< 10 GB):** Evaluation + optional/gated conversation sets and personality traits (`lmsys_chat_1m`, `mbti_personality_traits`).
- **`ml_persona` (independent, opt-in):** One ~245 MB pinned Nemotron shard; retain 6,000 hash-ranked synthetic adult profiles. Not included in any legacy profile, including `full`. All legacy entries retain `training_allowed=False`.

## Evidence Roles

Each dataset has exactly one role in `bebshax/persona/evidence.py::DATASET_ROLES` — a data table, not filename luck:

| Role | Meaning | Datasets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grounding` | Real-world records personas may cite as OBSERVED evidence | `amazon_reviews_office_products`, `empathetic_dialogues_slice`, `mbti_personality_traits` |
| `seed` | Synthetic diversity sketches — generation perspective only, NEVER citable | `personahub_sample` |
| `dialogue_examples` | Synthetic/LLM-generated conversations — never evidence | `synthetic_persona_chat`, `lmsys_chat_1m` |
| `probe` | Routing/capability checks — never evidence | `mmlu_micro`, `gsm8k_micro`, `router_arena`, `xroute_bench` |
| `training` | Reviewed synthetic priors for isolated non-LLM training; excluded from EvidenceStore and generation seeds, NEVER OBSERVED | `nemotron_personas_usa_ml` |

Unknown dataset files default to `grounding` (the drop-a-real-corpus-in extension path); every managed dataset above must have an explicit row — test-enforced.

## Coverage & Representativeness

These datasets are **slices, not representative samples**: they skew toward specific domains (office-product reviews, English-language dialogues, exam-style probes) and specific populations. No conclusion drawn from them — or from personas grounded in them — generalizes to 'all users'. BebshaX produces synthetic research participants; synthetic findings are research signals and hypotheses, not ground truth. Validate consequential decisions with real users.

## Reproducible Non-LLM Training Input

```text
python scripts/setup_datasets.py --profile ml_persona
python scripts/setup_datasets.py --profile ml_persona --verify-only
python scripts/setup_datasets.py --profile ml_persona --force
```

Only official `hf_hub_download` and `HfApi.get_paths_info` are used, pinned to the manifest revision with `token=False`. The pinned README card license must equal `cc-by-4.0` before the shard is downloaded. Its primary raw SHA-256 and size must equal the upstream LFS metadata; all mismatches fail closed. `--force` passes `force_download=True` for both the ML card and shard. Legacy download behavior is unchanged.

The sampler scans every row group with existing `fastparquet`, retains the lowest 6,000 SHA-256 ranks of UTF-8 `<hf_repo_id>@<pinned_revision>:<uuid>`, and sorts output by hash then UUID. No prefix or random sampling. It keeps at most the selected row payloads plus one row group and UUID identifiers; all allowed narratives are copied in full, without whitespace stripping or truncation. JSONL is UTF-8 with LF newlines. Sex, zipcode, list variants and all other unapproved columns are excluded. Invalid/duplicate UUIDs and empty shards are fatal; other missing/invalid fields are reported for strict downstream normalization, not silently repaired.

The processed file is `data/processed/nemotron_personas_usa_ml.jsonl`; its explicit training role prevents evidence ingestion. Raw shard/card files live under `data/raw/nemotron_personas_usa_ml/`. Metadata is `data/metadata/nemotron_personas_usa_ml.json`: manifest projection plus `metadata_version`, `artifact_role`, `actual_raw_sha256`, `upstream_raw_sha256`, `actual_processed_sha256`, `actual_card_sha256`, canonical `metadata_sha256`, sizes, record count, `license_check` and `inspection`. The inspection records scanned/selected counts, row groups, selection recipe, columns, missing/invalid counts and maximum text lengths. It is not a population audit.

`--verify-only` for ML is strictly local and read-only: metadata checksum, pinned source/revision, card/license, raw LFS hash, output hash, sizes and record count must all match. Missing or mismatched artifacts fail; an intact output alone cannot produce a skip. Metadata hashing detects corruption, not a cryptographic signature against coordinated local tampering. After the first approved download, main may pin the recorded raw/output checksums in the manifest. Splits and model artifacts belong under `data/processed/ml_persona/` and are not created by ingestion.

`ml_persona/SOURCES.json` is generated deterministically from the manifest, not hand-maintained. `get_manifest()` preserves the ten-entry legacy catalog; documentation explicitly requests `get_manifest(include_training=True)` to include the independent training catalog. The standalone downloader imports no `ml_persona` package. No new install is needed: Hub, fastparquet and pydantic already exist; PyYAML is an existing required Hub dependency used only to catch its structured-card parser errors, not a new parser or package installation.

### Field Mapping And Limits

Raw-shaped rows are handed to `prepare_records(rows, source, revision)` by the package owner: `age`, `occupation`, `education_level`, `city`, `state`, `country` retain their source meanings; `uuid` identifies the synthetic profile. `career_goals_and_ambitions` supplies goal candidates; regex `pain_points` extraction from full narratives yields hypotheses, not ground truth labels. Income is absent and remains unknown; never infer budget from age or occupation. Ingestion adds no income, budget, demographic prediction or supervised labels.

Attribution: NVIDIA Corporation, Nemotron-Personas-USA v1.1 (2025-10-28; repository updated 2025-12-16), CC-BY-4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The hash-ranked subset and allowlisted field projection are BebshaX modifications; retain upstream attribution, license and change notices. Synthetic names in the upstream narratives are not real-user PII. The corpus covers USA adults and inherits generator, cultural and demographic biases. No Bangladesh population claims, no consumer-demand claims, and no empirical representativeness claims are supported.
