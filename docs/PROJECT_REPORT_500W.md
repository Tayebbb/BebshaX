## BebshaX — Evidence-Grounded Synthetic Market Research on a $0 AI Budget

**Problem.** User research is the most skipped step in building a product: recruiting 20 interview participants takes weeks, agencies charge thousands of dollars, and a student founder in Dhaka can afford neither. The shortcut — "just ask ChatGPT" — produces sycophantic, ungrounded answers with no way to tell real claims from invented ones.

**Solution.** BebshaX lets a founder pressure-test a business idea in minutes. The founder describes the idea in a chat; the system researches the market, imports and segments open datasets, and generates a panel of evidence-grounded synthetic customers — realistic personas with local demographics and BDT-denominated budgets. The founder then interviews these personas (they stay in character, remember earlier answers, keep numeric claims consistent, and push back like real customers), runs behavioral tests such as pricing and feature A/B tests across the panel, and receives a versioned validation report. Every report ends with a "validate with real customers next" handoff: BebshaX is a rigorous rehearsal before real research, never a replacement.

**Engineering.** The project runs on a genuinely zero API budget, which is also its research question: _can intelligent multi-model routing aggregate free LLM capacity while preserving persona quality and user experience?_ A custom policy layer routes 18 task types across seven model pools spanning ~18 free providers, with context budgeting, per-pool concurrency, quota ledgers, cooldowns, and a closed 13-kind failure taxonomy — falling back to a local Ollama model on the team's own GPU, so the full workflow runs with no internet and no API keys. We observed the thesis live: during the 2026-08 end-to-end audits (an unstored smoke run) an interview survived four different providers failing and swapping mid-conversation without the persona breaking character, and a cross-route persona-consistency evaluation now measures this systematically. Honesty is enforced in code, not trusted to the AI: every persona claim is labeled OBSERVED, INFERRED, or SYNTHETIC; fabricated citations are mechanically detected and downgraded; demo fixtures are permanently labeled CACHED. The system comprises a FastAPI backend (~117 endpoints, PostgreSQL + pgvector) and a React frontend, verified by 470+ deterministic backend tests, including chaos tests of every failover path.

**SDG alignment.** BebshaX serves SDG 8 (target 8.3) by removing the cost and time barriers that stop student entrepreneurs from validating ideas; SDG 9 (9.b) by contributing reusable zero-budget AI routing infrastructure grounded in open data (World Bank live API, BBS-modeled catalogs); and SDG 10 (10.2) by making a tool normally reserved for funded startups available to anyone — Bangladesh-first by data, not hardcoding.

**Complex Engineering Problem.** The build satisfies the CEP attributes: deep cross-domain knowledge (distributed failover, applied NLP, evaluation methodology — WP1); conflicting requirements resolved by written policy — zero budget versus never silently degrading quality (WP2); no established solution for mid-conversation provider failover with identity preservation (WP3, WP4); self-authored normative rules enforced by tests where no standard exists (WP5); stakeholders from providers' fair-use terms to the real populations whose statistics ground personas (WP6); and fifteen interdependent subsystems where provenance, routing, memory, and evaluation propagate into each other (WP7).

**Team.** Built by a three-member AUST team; all engineering decisions, quality gates, and audit reports are documented in the repository.
